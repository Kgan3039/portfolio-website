### After (Specific & Confident):

---

## ✨ Why These Changes Work:

### 1. **Action-Oriented Opening**
- ❌ Before: "I build..." (present continuous - vague)
- ✅ After: "I ship code that scales." (concrete, confident)
- **Impact**: Immediately shows you deliver results, not just write code

### 2. **Specific Achievements**
- ❌ Before: "explore AI, machine learning"
- ✅ After: "Built Firebase backends handling thousands of users at FiPet"
- **Impact**: Concrete numbers and real companies beat generic buzzwords

### 3. **Proof Points**
- ❌ Before: Generic statements about passion
- ✅ After: Three specific accomplishments:
  - Production backend at FiPet (shows professional experience)
  - ML models for stock prediction (shows technical depth)
  - Hackathon wins (shows execution speed)

### 4. **Clear Call-to-Action**
- ❌ Before: No mention of availability
- ✅ After: "Seeking Summer 2025 SWE internships" (bold, at the end)
- **Impact**: Recruiters know exactly what you want

### 5. **Concise Tagline**
- ❌ Before: Full sentence tagline
- ✅ After: "CS + Data Science @ UW–Madison · Backend Engineer · ML Enthusiast"
- **Impact**: Quick scan reveals your identity and focus areas

## 📝 Writing Tips Used:

1. **Start with action verbs**: "Ship", "Built", "Trained", "Won"
2. **Be specific**: "Firebase backends" not "backend systems"
3. **Use numbers**: "thousands of users" not "many users"
4. **Show progression**: Internship → Hackathons → ML Projects
5. **End with CTA**: Always close with what you want
6. **Kill fluff**: Remove "passionate", "explore", "interested in"
7. **Front-load impact**: Best stuff in first line
